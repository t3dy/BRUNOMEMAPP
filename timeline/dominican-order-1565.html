Enters the Dominican Order at San Domenico Maggiore, Naples

1565MEDIUM

Naples

Bruno joins the Dominican order and takes the name Giordano, beginning formal training in a house with a strong scholastic and mnemonic-pedagogical tradition.

Memory / Magic Connection Dominican formation included structured memory training as part of standard intellectual discipline; this is the institutional seedbed for Bruno's later obsessive elaboration of the art of memory into a full metaphysical-magical system.
