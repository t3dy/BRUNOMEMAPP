Publishes De imaginum, signorum et idearum compositione in Frankfurt

1591MEDIUM

Frankfurt

Bruno publishes his final and most theoretically mature treatise on images, alongside the three Latin didactic poems (De triplici minimo, De monade, De immenso).

Memory / Magic Connection The theoretical capstone of the entire memory-magic project — the fullest statement of how image, sign, and idea relate, written just before Bruno's return to Italy and arrest. Represents roughly a decade of development since De umbris idearum.
