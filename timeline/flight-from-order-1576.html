Flees the Dominican Order under suspicion of heresy

1576MEDIUM

Naples / Rome

Facing charges related to unorthodox reading and views (including on the Arian controversy), Bruno flees the order and begins over a decade of wandering across Europe.

Memory / Magic Connection Begins the itinerant period during which Bruno composes essentially all of his mnemonic and magical works (De umbris, Cantus Circaeus, the Seals texts, the Italian dialogues, De imaginum) — the biographical condition (constant travel, need to demonstrate intellectual value quickly to patrons) shaped the practical, performative dimension of his memory art as a marketable skill as well as a philosophical system.
