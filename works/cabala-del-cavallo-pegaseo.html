Cabala del cavallo pegaseo con l'aggiunta dell'Asino cillenico

The Cabala of the Pegasean Horse, with the Addition of the Cyllenic Ass
ITALIAN_DIALOGUE 1585 MAJOR HIGH

Italian · published 1585 in Paris

A satirical dialogue mocking both Jewish Kabbalistic exegesis and the pretensions of learned ignorance, built around an extended, deliberately absurd celebration of the Ass as a symbol of divine and asinine wisdom simultaneously. One of Bruno's most consciously comic works.

Memory / Magic Connection Bruno's own combinatorial/exegetical method (letter-manipulation, numerological wordplay, image-multiplication) is the SAME toolkit used seriously in De umbris and De imaginum, here turned on itself for satire. It is direct primary evidence that Bruno himself treated his own symbolic-combinatorial apparatus as something that could be played with, inflated, and laughed at — which licenses building a genuinely comic app mode without inventing a tone Bruno never used.

Notable Content

The Ass as universal symbol; parody of Kabbalistic letter-combination; Pegasus/asinine wisdom inversion
