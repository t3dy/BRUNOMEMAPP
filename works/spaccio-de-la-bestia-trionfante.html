Spaccio de la bestia trionfante

The Expulsion of the Triumphant Beast
ITALIAN_DIALOGUE 1584 SUPPORTING MEDIUM

Italian · published 1584 in London

An allegorical dialogue in which the Olympian gods vote to expel vices (personified as constellations) and replace them with virtues, as part of a general ethical and cosmic reformation.

Memory / Magic Connection Clucas explicitly reads this alongside the technical mnemonic works: the dialogue DRAMATIZES the same ethical reordering of the soul's operations (appetite, desire, disposition) that the memory art is supposed to accomplish technically. The constellation-imagery reform is a macrocosmic mirror of the microcosmic reordering the mnemonic images perform in an individual soul.

Notable Content

Constellation reform as ethical allegory; virtues replacing vices
