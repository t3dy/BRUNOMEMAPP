De la causa, principio et uno

On Cause, Principle and Unity
ITALIAN_DIALOGUE 1584 SUPPORTING MEDIUM

Italian · published 1584 in London

Metaphysical dialogue on the relation between the universal cause, the formative principle, and the underlying unity of all things — Bruno's most sustained statement of his monist metaphysics.

Memory / Magic Connection Supplies the metaphysics of unity-into-multiplicity that Clucas and Farinella/Preston argue the memory wheel operationalizes: the wheel's movement from single letters to combined images mirrors the descent from the One into the many described philosophically here.
