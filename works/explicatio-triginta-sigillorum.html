Explicatio triginta sigillorum

Explanation of the Thirty Seals
MAGICAL_TEXT 1583 CENTRAL MEDIUM

Latin · published 1583 in London

Thirty 'seals' (sigilli) — structured mental/magical operations — presented as techniques for organizing thought, memory, and will. Published alongside Ars reminiscendi and the short Sigillus sigillorum.

Memory / Magic Connection The seals are explicitly named techniques, several of which are mnemonic in function and several explicitly magical/operative. This text is where 'memory technique' and 'magical operation' are hardest to pull apart — Bruno presents them in the same numbered list.
