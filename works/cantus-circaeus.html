Cantus Circaeus

Circe's Incantation
MNEMONIC_TREATISE 1582 CENTRAL MEDIUM

Latin · published 1582 in Paris

A dialogue framing mnemonic technique through Circe casting spells with mirrors and incantations to summon images. Companion piece to De umbris idearum, published the same year.

Memory / Magic Connection Makes the magical framing of memory training explicit and literal: the mnemonic apparatus is presented AS a magical operation (Circe's magic), not merely illustrated by one. Central primary evidence for the Yatesian 'memory as magic' reading, and a text any de-esotericizing reading (Barenstein) has to explain away or reinterpret.

Notable Content

Circe as figure for the mnemonic operator; mirror/incantation apparatus
