De compendiosa architectura et complemento artis Lullii

On the Compendious Architecture and Complement of the Art of Lull
MNEMONIC_TREATISE 1582 MAJOR MEDIUM

Latin · published 1582 in Paris

Bruno's engagement with Ramon Llull's combinatorial Ars Magna, adapting Llull's rotating concentric letter-wheels for logical/theological combination into Bruno's own memory system.

Memory / Magic Connection This is the direct technical ancestor of the De umbris memory wheel's rotating-ring mechanism. Essential for understanding the wheel as a Lullian combinatorial engine, not only (or even primarily) a Hermetic talisman — relevant evidence for the Sturlese/Torchia correction.
