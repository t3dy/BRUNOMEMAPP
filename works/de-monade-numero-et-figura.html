De monade, numero et figura

On the Monad, Number and Figure
LATIN_POEM 1591 SUPPORTING MEDIUM

Latin · published 1591 in Frankfurt

Second Frankfurt poem, on number and geometric figure as expressions of the monad (unity).

Memory / Magic Connection Farinella and Preston's argument that Bruno gives mathematics a qualitative, not merely quantitative, function draws directly on this text — relevant to why the memory wheel's geometry (circles, divisions) is treated as philosophically meaningful rather than merely a convenient diagram layout.

Notable Content

Qualitative mathematics; monad/number/figure theory
