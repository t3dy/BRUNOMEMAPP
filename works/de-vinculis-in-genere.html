De vinculis in genere

On Bonds in General
MAGICAL_TEXT c. 1590-91 CENTRAL HIGH

Latin · published posthumous

A treatise on vincula (bonds) — the psychological/magical ties that connect a 'binder' to a 'bound' subject through imagination, desire, and attention. Analyzes love, persuasion, and magical influence as structurally the same operation.

Memory / Magic Connection THE key text for Couliano's reading of Bruno as a theorist of psychological binding via phantasia and desire. Directly underwrites the proposed 'Vinculum / Bond Mode' — the vincula described here are the explicit primary-source basis for that entire interactive mode.

Notable Content

Theory of vincula (bonds); mechanics of attention/desire/imagination as binding forces
