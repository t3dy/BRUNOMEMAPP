Sigillus sigillorum

The Seal of Seals
MAGICAL_TEXT 1583 CENTRAL MEDIUM

Latin · published 1583 in London

A short capstone text describing a master operation that governs and unifies the Thirty Seals — a technique for controlling the internal senses (common sense, imagination, memory) as a single coordinated system.

Memory / Magic Connection Directly proposes a unified psychological/magical faculty-control technique. Central primary source for the 'Seal Lab' interactive mode and for questions about whether Bruno's magic ultimately targets the operator's own cognitive architecture rather than external forces.
