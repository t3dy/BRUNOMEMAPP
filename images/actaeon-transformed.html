Actaeon Transformed by the Sight of Diana

MYTHOLOGICAL_FIGUREHIGH

From De gli eroici furori

Actaeon, hunting, glimpses Diana bathing and is transformed into a stag, then torn apart by his own hounds.

What It DoesFunctions as the master image for contemplative transformation: the one who truly sees the object of contemplation is remade by the seeing, and consumed by his own faculties (the hounds = his own thoughts/desires) once transformed. Not merely illustrative — Bruno uses it as the operative model for what any sufficiently potent image is supposed to do to its beholder.
