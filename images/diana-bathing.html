From De gli eroici furori

Diana, goddess of the hunt and the moon, bathing unseen (until Actaeon intrudes) — the object of contemplation in the Actaeon myth.

What It DoesRepresents the object of philosophical/erotic contemplation itself — truth or divine unity as something that transforms whoever truly perceives it, rather than a passive fact to be recorded.
