The Ass (Pegasean / Cyllenic)

ANIMALHIGH

From Cabala del cavallo pegaseo con l'aggiunta dell'Asino cillenico

A deliberately absurd composite symbol: the ass elevated to cosmic/divine significance, punning on Pegasus (winged horse of poetic inspiration) and Cyllene (Hermes' birthplace, patron of hidden wisdom).

What It DoesSatirizes the exact combinatorial-symbolic method Bruno uses seriously elsewhere: take a humble/absurd term, run it through etymological and mythological combination, and inflate it into cosmic significance. It is Bruno's own toolkit turned into a joke on itself.

Reconstruction Level

HISTORICALLY_ATTESTED

Scholarly Interpretation

Primary evidence that Bruno's symbolic-combinatorial method was consciously available to him as comic material, not only solemn doctrine.
