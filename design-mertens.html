Frame: MERTENS

Type: interpretive frame · Attributed to: Manuel Mertens (Magic and Memory in Giordano Bruno: The Art of a Heroic Spirit, 2018) · Artifacts: Wheel, Image Lab, Seals, Vincula, Frenzies

Provenance warning. LLM_GENERAL_KNOWLEDGE. See ARCHITECTURE.md §4.

Stance in one line

Memory and magic are not two Bruno projects but one, sharing philosophical foundations: mnemonic technique regulates the spirits involved in cognition — and can protect cognition from demonic corruption.

The triple

MechanismInherits Sturlese's corrected wheel, plus an explicit protective/regulatory framing of the operation.
OutputSpirit-regulated cognition — a mind ordered against interference.
Success criterionResistance to cognitive interference and distraction.
TestabilityT1 (partial), with a hard T3 residue.

What this frame changes about the word "magic"

Mertens shifts the operative question from can this image attract planetary power? to how does an image regulate the forces involved in cognition? That relocation is what makes magic and memory one subject rather than two, and it is a different route to that conclusion than Clucas takes — Clucas gets there through ethics and Plotinus, Mertens through spirit-theory and the heroic transformation of the operator.

The consequence for the portal: the simulacrum stops being an occult decal and becomes an instrument with a job. That is a better basis for an Image Lab than either the Yatesian talisman or the bare mnemonic hook.

The interference test (partial T1)

"Protection of cognition" has one honest measurable shadow: encoding and recall performance under deliberate interference.

  1. Baseline recall via the wheel, quiet conditions.
  2. Repeat under imposed interference — competing task, distractor stream, time pressure.
  3. Measure the degradation delta, and whether the wheel-encoded material degrades less than control material.

If Mertens's regulatory reading has any cognitive purchase, structured Brunian encoding should degrade more gracefully than unstructured memorisation. That is checkable.

What this explicitly does not test: demonic corruption. The app measures interference resistance and says so in those words. It must never let a distractor-task result be read as evidence about demons — that would be the T1/T3 register collapse the architecture forbids. The demonic frame is presented as historical reconstruction, unscored.

Interactive behaviour

  • Corrected wheel; switcher notes shared mechanism.
  • A protective-framing overlay: the historical account of what the operation was understood to guard against, presented as reconstruction with primary citations, no output panel.
  • The interference harness, reported strictly as interference resistance.
  • Cross-link to the heroic spirit material in FRENZIES.md — Mertens is the frame that makes the Eroici furori and the technical treatises legible as one project alongside Clucas.

Primary texts

  • De magia (c. 1590) — the taxonomy and the spiritus / species causal vocabulary
  • Theses de magia, De rerum principiis — the posthumous magical notebooks
  • Sigillus sigillorum — internal-senses control, the closest thing to a regulatory technique
  • De gli eroici furori — the heroic spirit
  • Mertens (2018)

Without the posthumous magical works, the "magic" in Bruno's memory art has no stated mechanism attached; this frame is the main consumer of those texts in the portal.

Disputes

  • esoteric-vs-nonesoteric-interpretation — synthetic tendency, against Barenstein's deflation
  • Introduces a live question the seed does not yet model: is Mertens's route to memory-magic unity compatible with Clucas's, or a rival to it? Both reject the binary; they do not obviously agree on why. Worth a new dispute row.

Build notes

  • References Sturlese's mechanism_spec with an additive framing layer.
  • Interference harness is another parameterisation of the Sturlese recall harness. Third frame, still no new measurement machinery — further argument for building that harness well once.

To verify in corpus

  • Does "protection from demonic corruption" carry a technical mechanism, or is it framing language? Determines whether the regulatory reading has operational content at all.
  • Mertens's account of the relation between memory technique and deification.
  • Whether Mertens engages Clucas directly — needed before asserting agreement or rivalry on the site.
