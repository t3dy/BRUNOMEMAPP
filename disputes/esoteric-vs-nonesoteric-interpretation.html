After the Sturlese/Torchia correction, is Bruno's memory art fundamentally magical, fundamentally cognitive/technical, or is that distinction itself the wrong frame?

ONGOINGHIGH

Julián Barenstein

The magical/Hermetic interpretation has been overextended. De umbris idearum's mechanics and cognitive theory deserve reconstruction on their own terms, as memoria regulata — disciplined, regulated memory intelligible without an esoteric frame.

Stephen Clucas

The scientific-memory-technique vs. occult-magic binary is itself too crude for Bruno. His mnemonic art is simultaneously logic, ethics, and magic — ordering the operations of the soul (intellect AND appetite together). Reducing it to 'merely mnemonic' loses exactly what makes it Brunian.

Resolution Note

Ostojić frames this as a live historiographical problem: the ars memoriae tends to get classified according to whichever Bruno (proto-scientist vs. mystic) the interpreter already believes in. Mertens and Wang offer a third path — magic as spirit-regulation / iconic epistemology — that doesn't map cleanly onto either side.
