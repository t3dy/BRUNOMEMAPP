Is Bruno's psychology of memory/imagination fundamentally Plotinian, Aristotelian, or an unstable mixture?

ONGOINGMEDIUM

Stephen Clucas

Bruno's higher art derives essentially from Plotinus's theory that memory/soul is an active power, not a passive receiver of impressions (rejecting the wax-tablet model), mediated to Bruno through Ficino.

Position B

Recent scholarship (per the essay 'plotinus-bruno-memory' in this corpus) argues Bruno combines Plotinian and Aristotelian elements inseparably: Aristotelian psychology supplies the necessity of images/phantasms for human cognition, while Plotinian metaphysics supplies the soul-as-active-power and unity-to-multiplicity structure. It is often impossible to assign a single source to a given psychological claim in Bruno.

Resolution Note

Not a clean two-scholar dispute so much as an open synthesis question; flagged here because it's load-bearing for how 'Plotinian Memory Mode' should be designed (pure Plotinus vs. Bruno's actual hybrid).
