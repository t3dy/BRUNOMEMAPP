Where do the planetary images belong in the De umbris idearum memory wheel, and does the wheel function as a magical talisman?

CONSENSUSHIGH

Frances A. Yates

Yates's reconstruction placed the planetary figures such that the wheel, once internalized, functioned as an inner talisman — a Hermetic-magical instrument for drawing down astral influence into the practitioner's soul.

Rita Sturlese

Sturlese's 1991 critical edition, extended by Torchia (1997), showed the planetary images were actually placed differently. The corrected wheel emphasizes combinatorial phonetic function (consonant-vowel combinations producing memorable syllable-images) rather than talismanic operation. The Warburg Institute now treats Yates's talisman reading of the wheel specifically as untenable.

Resolution Note

The specific claim (this wheel = a talisman by this mechanism) is superseded. What remains contested is the larger question the correction reopened: whether 'magic' should be dropped from the interpretation of Bruno's memory art altogether, or reconceived (Clucas, Mertens) rather than discarded. See dispute: esoteric-vs-nonesoteric-interpretation.
