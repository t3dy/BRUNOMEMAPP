The Art Engine

Figure S: the state of the practitioner's rational soul. Not the static powers of memory, intellect and will, but their ACTS and combinations of acts -- Bonner: 'a figure that begins by being dynamic and combinatory.' Four species (E I N R), each a triple of one act per power.

Whose art is this?

Llull's Art, quaternary phase — not Bruno's. Bonner mentions Bruno only twice, for the cosmological use of the combinatorics. Blum reports that Bruno's De compendiosa architectura recasts Lullian logic in Aristotelian terms; Mertens reports that what returns in Bruno is the similitudo-logic, not the letter notation. No evidence in this corpus that Bruno adopted Figure S. See docs/design/ENGINE.md section 1a.

Bruno's version: Bruno's adaptation is a logica fantastica -- a logic whose operands are images held in the spiritus phantasticus, rather than Llull's letter-variables. That is why he must first build an image vocabulary (the atria and the image alphabet).

The aim of the Art

Bonner, on the first two compartments: the aim of the Art is to get the user -- 'or rather the S of the user' -- to remember, understand and love (E) God, the virtues and the truth, while remembering, understanding and hating (I) the vices and falsehood. The remaining states are unreliable belief (N) or confusion (R). The machine's output is a change in the operator.

Figura S

The readout reports the state of the figure, never of the reader. Reaching E in the model is a fact about the model.

Predestinatio et liberum arbitrium Predestination and free will

God's wisdom is perfect, and perfect wisdom foreknows all things. But if all is foreknown, what becomes of free will? Work the contradiction through the four figures.
