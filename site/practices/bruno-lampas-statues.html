Bruno's Thirty Statues

Bruno 1587 partially operable corpus-grounded MEDIUM

Lampas triginta statuarum (Thirty Statues, trans. as 'a book of the art of memory')

Not images for recalling a list, but conceptual figures for generating what there is to say about anything.

What you can actually do Take a subject, run it through a statue's numbered notions, and generate the full range of things sayable about it. This is an art of finding, not of retaining.
Before you start

This is the practice most likely to feel like a bait-and-switch, and it is the most important one to understand. You came for a memory palace and you have been handed the scale of nature. Bruno's Statue of Venus is not a picture of Venus to help you recall something — it is the topic of concordance-according-to-will, subdivided into thirty numbered notions: aptitude, facility, compliance, complacence, delectation, adoption, consensus, and so on. The system organises concepts rather than storing items. In the older vocabulary this is inventio, the art of finding what to say, and it sits closer to logic than to mnemonics. That gap is real history, not a broken interface.

Operability

The statues and their numbered subdivisions are given, and can be run as a topic generator. But Bruno's metaphysical scale is stated far more fully than any procedure for using it, so much of the 'how' is reconstruction.

The Practice

  1. 1

    Accept the change of purpose

    reconstructed

    Stop trying to memorise a list. You are going to take a subject and systematically generate everything that can be said about it.

    Our framing of the Prologue's account of investigation.

    Worldview

    “This is not what 'art of memory' was supposed to mean.”

    Memory, invention and logic were not separate faculties with separate techniques for Bruno. An ordered store of topics is simultaneously a thing you remember and a thing you think with, so a system that arranges concepts well is doing memory work by definition. The modern split — mnemonics over here, brainstorming over there, logic somewhere else — is the anachronism, not Bruno's fusion.

  2. 2

    Choose a statue

    attested

    Each statue is a topic — a mode under which things can be considered. Venus is concordance according to will; others cover other modes.

    Thirty Statues, 'Statue of Venus' (line ~4078)

    Interface

    “I am told to choose a statue, but only Venus is spelled out in any detail.”

    That is the state of the source as we have it here. Venus is worked through at length; the others are named and sketched. Until the rest are harvested you can genuinely run one topic-generator, not thirty — which is why this practice is graded partially operable. Better to have one that works than thirty invented ones.

  3. 3

    Run the numbered notions

    attested

    Work through the statue's subdivisions in order, asking what each yields for your subject. Under Venus: aptitude, then facility, then compliance, complacence, delectation, adoption, consensus, excitation, approbation, observation, attention, and onward.

    Thirty Statues (line ~4100 ff.)

    Interface

    “The notions are abstract to the point of being slippery. What is the difference between complacence and delectation?”

    The distinctions are scholastic and fine-grained, and the translation is working hard. Delectation is given as the limit of complacence — the point at which pleasure in a thing is complete. Expect to spend real time on the vocabulary before the generator produces anything useful, and expect some distinctions to stay blurry. This is why the practice is graded partially operable.

  4. 4

    Locate on the scale of nature

    attested

    Place your subject on the thirty-degree ladder that runs from vacuum, shadow, matter and atom at the bottom toward the highest simple things — and read off what its position implies.

    Thirty Statues, 'The Scale of Nature' (line ~4750)

    Worldview

    “Why would where something sits on a metaphysical ladder tell me anything about it?”

    Because for Bruno the ladder is not a filing convention but the actual structure of things, descending from unity into multiplicity. Knowing a subject's degree tells you what it participates in and therefore what can truly be said of it. Take the metaphysics away and the procedure becomes an arbitrary sorting exercise — which is exactly what makes this practice hard to run without at least provisionally inhabiting the worldview.
