Planetary Figures Ring

From De umbris idearum

A ring of the memory wheel bearing figures associated with the seven classical planets.

What It DoesDisputed. Yates read this ring's placement as making the wheel function as an inner talisman drawing down astral influence. Sturlese and Torchia's corrected placement undermines that specific mechanism, though the ring's general cosmological-correspondence function is not in dispute.
