Alessandro Farinella & Carole Preston

MEDIUM

Interpretation Summary Their 2002 article situates the mnemonic wheel within Bruno's philosophical synthesis, arguing Bruno incorporates Ficinian Neoplatonic metaphysics and integrates it with other structures to produce new memory techniques. Emphasize Bruno's attempt to give mathematics a qualitative, not merely quantitative, function — the wheel's circularity, divisions, and hierarchical relations are themselves philosophical assertions about reality's structure, not just a convenient graphic organizer.
