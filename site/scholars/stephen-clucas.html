Stephen Clucas

HIGH

Interpretation Summary Accepts that Yates's reconstruction needs correction but argues strongly against reducing Bruno's art to sophisticated ordinary memory training. His key formulation: Bruno's mnemonic art is simultaneously logic, ethics, and magic — organizing the operations of the soul (intellect AND appetite), not merely storing information. Reads Bruno's Italian dialogues and technical mnemonic writings as sharing one project: harnessing intellect and imagination to transform ethical and intellectual capacities ('domestication of the soul').

Memory

Memory is an activity that orders the operations of the soul, per Plotinus mediated through Ficino — not passive storage but active reorganization of intellect and appetite together.

Imagination

Imagination embodies universal relations grasped by intellect in memorable, psychologically operative form; necessary complement to intellect for embodied cognition.

Images

Images (simulacra, signacula) participate in an ordered system of relations through which intellect and imagination operate; not labels attached to information.

Magic

Magic is one of three complementary dimensions (with logic and ethics) of a single project of human perfection/transformation; concerns the efficacy of images and the bonds they establish between things, not necessarily supernatural force transmission.

Neoplatonism

Central. Bruno's higher art derives essentially from Neoplatonic theory of knowledge, especially Plotinus, mediated through Ficino.

Plotinus

Foundational. Plotinus's rejection of the wax-tablet model (memory as active power of soul, not passive impression) underlies Bruno's whole project; training memory strengthens the soul itself.

Ficino

The mediating channel through which Plotinian psychology reaches Bruno.

Mnemonic Wheels

The wheel operates at multiple simultaneous levels: syllabic/mnemonic, intellectual (concepts), philosophical (relations among forms), psychological (attention), ethical (desire), and potentially mystical (return toward source).

Seals / Simulacra

Simulacra and signacula are instruments for ordering the soul's operations; discussed at length in 'Simulacra et Signacula: Memory, Magic and Metaphysics in Brunian Mnemonics.'
