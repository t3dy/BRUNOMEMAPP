Rita Sturlese

HIGH

Interpretation Summary Her 1991 critical edition of De umbris idearum showed Yates had misplaced the planetary images in the wheel reconstruction. The corrected wheel emphasizes combinatorial phonetic function — consonant-vowel combinations producing syllables attached to memorable images — making the wheel look more like an elaborate symbolic encoding system than a talisman.

Memory

Primarily a combinatorial-phonetic encoding system: syllables generated by ring rotation, attached to memorable images.

Images

Images are mnemonic hooks for syllables/words generated combinatorially, not primarily magical instruments.

Magic

Skeptical of Yates's talismanic reading specifically; does not deny magic in Bruno generally, but the wheel's mechanism itself is not a talisman as reconstructed.

Mnemonic Wheels

Corrected planetary ring placement; wheel is a combinatorial phonetic machine, not (as reconstructed) a talisman.
