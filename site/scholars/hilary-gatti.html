Hilary Gatti

MEDIUM

Interpretation Summary Refuses to isolate Bruno's mnemonic writings from his larger philosophical development. Edited the volume containing Clucas's 'Simulacra et Signacula' chapter; her broader project situates the mnemonic work within Bruno's full philosophy, where images are simultaneously epistemological (how we know) and ontological (what kind of universe makes such knowledge possible).

Images

Bruno's images are simultaneously epistemological and ontological — concerned with both how we know and what the universe must be like for that knowing to be possible.
