Aleksandar Ostojić

MEDIUM

Interpretation Summary His 2020 article identifies two dominant historiographical traditions for interpreting Bruno — precursor of modern science vs. mystic/mage/Hermetic philosopher — and shows how the ars memoriae gets classified according to whichever Bruno the scholar already believes in. Argues for moving beyond the magic/science binary as a modern category that distorts the Renaissance configuration.

Magic

The magic/science binary is a modern historiographical imposition; Bruno's own configuration allows mathematics, philosophy, psychology, cosmology, and magic to operate together without contradiction.
