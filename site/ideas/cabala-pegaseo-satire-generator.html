The Cabala Generator: Build Your Own Sacred Ass

COMEDIC_GAME COMEDIC IDEA MEDIUM

A generator that lets the user take Bruno's own combinatorial-etymological method (letter manipulation, mythological punning, numerological inflation) and apply it to any mundane word or animal of their choosing, producing a mock-cosmic exegesis in Bruno's satirical register. Explicitly framed as a parody TOOL, not a claim about real Kabbalah.

Why this source supports it: Cabala del cavallo pegaseo is Bruno turning his own serious symbolic-combinatorial apparatus (the same one used in De umbris and De imaginum) on itself as satire of Kabbalistic exegesis and learned pretension. Building a generator that does this is not inventing a tone for Bruno -- it's letting the user operate the exact move Bruno already made.
