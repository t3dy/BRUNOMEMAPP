App-Mode Ideas

Interactive tool and game concepts, grounded in specific works — serious modes (Vinculum/Bond, Seal Lab, Actaeon’s Chamber) alongside explicitly comic ones for the Cabala del cavallo pegaseo, licensed by Bruno’s own use of satire.

The Cabala Generator: Build Your Own Sacred Ass

COMEDIC
COMEDIC_GAME
IDEA

A generator that lets the user take Bruno's own combinatorial-etymological method (letter manipulation, mythological punning, numerological inflation) and apply it to any mundane word or animal of their choosing, producing a mock-cosmic exegesis in Bruno's satirical register. Explicitly framed as a parody TOOL, not a claim about real Kabbalah.

The Cyllenic Ass Oracle

COMEDIC
COMEDIC_GAME
IDEA

A mock-oracular advice tool voiced as the Ass from the Cabala -- dispensing deliberately paradoxical 'asinine wisdom' (profound-sounding nonsense that mimics real oracular/Kabbalistic register) in answer to user questions, with a toggle to reveal the 'serious' Brunian doctrine each joke is parodying.

Same Bruno, Different Mood

PLAYFUL
COMPARISON_TOOL
IDEA

A comparison tool that takes a single Brunian concept (e.g. transformation-through-contemplation, or combinatorial symbol-inflation) and shows the SAME underlying move as it appears once played straight (Eroici furori's Actaeon) and once played for laughs (Cabala's Ass) -- making Bruno's range of register itself the object of study.

Actaeon's Chamber

SERIOUS
NARRATIVE_GAME
IDEA

A symbolic/psychological environment based on the Actaeon myth: the user enters a scene, chooses what to contemplate (an image, a concept, a passage), and the interface visibly 'transforms' around their choice of focus, dramatizing Wang's 'image as operation' and Clucas's 'domestication of the soul' as direct interaction rather than exposition.

Frenzy Sonnet + Gloss Explorer

SERIOUS
SCHOLARLY_SANDBOX
IDEA

Side-by-side reader for the Eroici furori sonnet cycle: each sonnet paired with Bruno's own prose commentary, plus togglable scholarly-interpretation layers (Clucas's ascent reading vs. Couliano's desire/bond reading) laid over the same lines.

Seal Lab

SERIOUS
IMAGE_TOOL
IDEA

Experimental interface for the Thirty Seals and the Seal of Seals, clearly distinguishing what Bruno explicitly specifies, what scholars reconstruct, and what the app invents for playability.

Vinculum / Bond Mode

SERIOUS
SCHOLARLY_SANDBOX
IDEA

Models relationships among image, attention, imagination, desire, emotion, person, object, and action per Couliano's reading of De vinculis in genere. Explicitly labeled COULIANO-INSPIRED EXPERIMENTAL MODEL; does not claim to reproduce supernatural magic.
