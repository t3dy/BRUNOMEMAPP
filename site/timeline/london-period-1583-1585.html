London period: Ars reminiscendi, the Seals texts, and the Italian dialogues

1583-1585MEDIUM

London

Under the patronage of the French ambassador, Bruno publishes Ars reminiscendi, Explicatio triginta sigillorum, Sigillus sigillorum, and the major Italian dialogues including Cabala del cavallo pegaseo, De gli eroici furori, and Spaccio de la bestia trionfante, dedicating works to Philip Sidney. His most productive period.

Memory / Magic Connection The single richest biographical window for this project: the technical Seals texts and the poetic/satirical Italian dialogues (Cabala, Furori) are written within roughly the same two years, by the same hand, for overlapping patrons — direct evidence that Bruno moved fluidly between solemn magical-technical register and comic-satirical register without treating them as separate projects.
