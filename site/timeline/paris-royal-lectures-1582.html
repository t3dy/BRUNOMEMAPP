1582MEDIUM

Paris

Bruno gains royal patronage in Paris, reportedly demonstrating feats of trained memory, and publishes his first cluster of mnemonic treatises.

Memory / Magic Connection The direct origin point of the whole memory-magic corpus this project studies. Bruno's memory art here functions simultaneously as scholarly system, court entertainment/demonstration, and (per Cantus Circaeus) explicitly magical performance — all three registers present in a single biographical moment.
