Timeline

Bruno’s life, filtered to events that bear specifically on his memory and magic system — not a general biography. Every entry states explicitly why it matters to that story.

1548

Born in Nola

Giordano Bruno (born Filippo Bruno) is born near Naples.

HIGH
1565

Enters the Dominican Order at San Domenico Maggiore, Naples

Bruno joins the Dominican order and takes the name Giordano, beginning formal training in a house with a strong scholastic and mnemonic-pedagogical tradition.

MEDIUM
1576

Flees the Dominican Order under suspicion of heresy

Facing charges related to unorthodox reading and views (including on the Arian controversy), Bruno flees the order and begins over a decade of wandering across Europe.

MEDIUM
1582

Lectures on memory before Henri III and the University of Paris; publishes De umbris idearum, Cantus Circaeus, De compendiosa architectura

Bruno gains royal patronage in Paris, reportedly demonstrating feats of trained memory, and publishes his first cluster of mnemonic treatises.

MEDIUM
1583-1585

London period: Ars reminiscendi, the Seals texts, and the Italian dialogues

Under the patronage of the French ambassador, Bruno publishes Ars reminiscendi, Explicatio triginta sigillorum, Sigillus sigillorum, and the major Italian dialogues including Cabala del cavallo pegaseo, De gli eroici furori, and Spaccio de la bestia trionfante, dedicating works to Philip Sidney. His most productive period.

MEDIUM
1586-1588

German period: Lampas triginta statuarum and continued mnemonic development

Bruno continues developing combinatorial-mnemonic systems, composing the Lampas triginta statuarum among other works during his years teaching and lecturing in German university towns.

LOW
1591

Publishes De imaginum, signorum et idearum compositione in Frankfurt

Bruno publishes his final and most theoretically mature treatise on images, alongside the three Latin didactic poems (De triplici minimo, De monade, De immenso).

MEDIUM
1592

Arrested by the Venetian Inquisition

Bruno is arrested after being denounced by his host Giovanni Mocenigo, who had sought to learn Bruno's memory and magical techniques and grew resentful/suspicious when unsatisfied.

HIGH
1600

Executed in Rome

After nearly eight years of imprisonment and trial by the Roman Inquisition, Bruno is burned at the stake for heresy.
