1586-1588LOW

Wittenberg / Helmstedt, Germany

Bruno continues developing combinatorial-mnemonic systems, composing the Lampas triginta statuarum among other works during his years teaching and lecturing in German university towns.

Memory / Magic Connection Shows the mature, later development of the 'thirty-unit' organizing principle first seen in the Thirty Intentions and Thirty Seals, now as Thirty Statues — evidence of a stable structural obsession across two decades of the memory-magic corpus.
