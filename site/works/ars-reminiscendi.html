Ars reminiscendi

The Art of Remembering
MNEMONIC_TREATISE 1583 CENTRAL MEDIUM

Latin · published 1583 in London

Published together with the Explicatio triginta sigillorum. A more procedural, technique-focused mnemonic manual than De umbris, closer to the classical ars memoriae tradition (loci and imagines).

Memory / Magic Connection The text closest to a plain mnemonic 'how-to,' and therefore the strongest evidence for de-esotericizing/cognitive readings of Bruno's memory art. Useful contrast case against Cantus Circaeus's magical framing.
