De gli eroici furori

The Heroic Frenzies
ITALIAN_DIALOGUE 1585 CENTRAL HIGH

Italian · published 1585 in London

A cycle of philosophical love-sonnets with prose commentary, dedicated to Philip Sidney, staging the soul's ascent through erotic/contemplative 'frenzy' toward union with the divine. Built around the myth of Actaeon transformed by the sight of Diana.

Memory / Magic Connection The Actaeon myth dramatizes, as narrative, the exact operation the mnemonic-magical images are theorized to perform elsewhere: being transformed by what one contemplates. Actaeon SEES Diana and is remade by the image. This is Wang's 'image as operation, not representation' and Clucas's 'domestication of the soul' acted out as story rather than stated as doctrine — the single best primary text for connecting the technical mnemonic treatises to lived psychological/spiritual transformation.

Notable Content

Actaeon and Diana; heroic furor as contemplative ascent; sonnet cycle with prose gloss
