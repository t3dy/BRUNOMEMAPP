De umbris idearum

On the Shadows of Ideas
MNEMONIC_TREATISE 1582 CENTRAL HIGH

Latin · published 1582 in Paris

Bruno's foundational mnemonic treatise. Presents the 'Thirty Intentions of the Shadows,' a theory of how images descend from unity into multiplicity, plus the concentric memory wheel combining letters, syllables, images, and planetary figures.

Memory / Magic Connection The central text of the whole project. Contains the wheel diagram whose planetary placement Yates and Sturlese/Torchia disagree about, and the 'higher art' passage (ordering the operations of the soul, including appetition) that grounds Clucas's logic+ethics+magic reading.

Notable Content

Thirty Intentions of the Shadows; the great memory wheel; concentric letter/image/planet system

Key Editions

  • Rita Sturlese (1991) — Critical edition; corrects Yates's planetary placement in the wheel
  • Frances Yates (1966) — Discussed at length in Giordano Bruno and the Hermetic Tradition, appendix reconstruction
