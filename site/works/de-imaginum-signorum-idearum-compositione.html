De imaginum, signorum et idearum compositione

On the Composition of Images, Signs, and Ideas
MAGICAL_TEXT 1591 CENTRAL HIGH

Latin · published 1591 in Frankfurt

Bruno's last and most theoretically developed treatise on images. Systematically addresses how images (imagines), signs (signa), and ideas (ideae) relate, and how composite images are constructed for memory, rhetoric, and magic simultaneously.

Memory / Magic Connection The single most important text for Zheng Wang's 'iconic practice' reading — image as instrument of knowing, not just storage. Also the fullest theoretical statement of how mnemonic, magical, and epistemological functions of the image are meant to relate.

Notable Content

Full theory of image/sign/idea composition; culmination of the mnemonic-magical corpus
