Lampas triginta statuarum

The Lamp of Thirty Statues
MNEMONIC_TREATISE 1587 MAJOR MEDIUM

Latin · published unpublished in Bruno's lifetime

A later, more elaborate combinatorial-mnemonic system using thirty personified 'statues' as organizing figures, written during Bruno's German period. Survives in manuscript, printed only posthumously.

Memory / Magic Connection Shows the mature development of Bruno's combinatorial approach after De umbris, and how the 'thirty-unit' organizing principle recurs across his mnemonic corpus (Intentions, Seals, Statues). Useful for tracing whether his system evolves toward or away from explicit magical framing over time.
