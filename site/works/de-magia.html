De magia

On Magic
MAGICAL_TEXT c. 1590 MAJOR MEDIUM

Latin · published posthumous

Part of the posthumously published 'magical works' notebooks from Bruno's Helmstedt/Frankfurt period. A systematic taxonomy of kinds of magic (natural, mathematical, demonic, etc.) and their operative principles.

Memory / Magic Connection Provides the theoretical vocabulary (species, spiritus, vinculum) that scholars use to explain HOW the mnemonic images are supposed to work causally. Without this text, the 'magic' in Bruno's memory art has no operative mechanism attached to it.

Notable Content

Taxonomy of magic; theory of spiritus and species as causal media
