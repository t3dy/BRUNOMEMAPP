Logica Fantastica

Llull's Art computes with letters. Bruno's computes with images, held in the spiritus phantasticus — which is why he had to build an image vocabulary before he could reason with it. Here the same dialectic runs on images composed from Bruno's own three systems.

Scholarly reconstruction

Similitudo-logic holds that the likeness of a thing carries real inferential weight -- principia essendi et cognoscendi are the same. If so, an image drawn from Saturn's court (whose attested retinue is the melancholic afflictions) genuinely inclines the soul differently from one drawn from Venus's (whose retinue is concord). That is the doctrine applied, not a mechanic invented. But Bruno does not say it in these words, and the label stays SCHOLARLY_RECONSTRUCTION.

Attested

  • Figure S and its four species, with the dialectic (Llull, via Bonner) -- see data/figure_s.json
  • The 24 atria and their 24 positions (Bruno, De imaginum Bk I Pt 2 chs 3-6) -- data/atria_harvested.json
  • The image alphabet: letter/cluster -> Latin agent-nouns (De imaginum chs 12-13) -- data/alphabet_harvested.json
  • The planetary courts and their retinues, including each court's affective character (De imaginum ~3590-4935) -- data/images_harvested.json
  • That Bruno's version of the Art is a logica fantastica whose operands are images in the spiritus phantasticus (Mertens; Rossi, 'La logica fantastica di Giordano Bruno')
  • That Bruno inherited from Llull a logic through similitudines, in which principia essendi et cognoscendi are the same (Mertens)

Reconstructed by this project

  • The combination. Bruno leaves no worked example of running a Lullian dialectic on composed images, so the wiring below -- operator to memory, atrium to intellect, court to will -- is this project's construction.
  • The specific claim that a court's affective register moves the WILL toward love or hatred. Each court's character is attested by its retinue; using that character as inferential force in a dialectic is our extension of the similitudo principle.

One power, one system

Each of the three powers of the soul is supplied by one of Bruno's three attested systems. Choose one of each to compose an image; the triple you produce determines which species of Figure S you land in.

memory

from the image alphabet

An operator drawn from the letter-key of your term gives the memory a handle to hold it by. Compose without one and the memory has nothing to grip: K, forgetting.

intellect

from the atria

Placing the image at an apt address means you have understood where in the order of things it belongs. Placing it arbitrarily means you are storing without knowing: L, not knowing.

will

from the planetary courts

The court's affective register inclines the will. Saturn's retinue is the melancholic afflictions; Venus's is concord. Draw from one and the will is moved toward hatred, from the other toward love.
