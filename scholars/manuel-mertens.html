Manuel Mertens

HIGH

Interpretation Summary His 2018 book untangles the relationship between Bruno's mnemonic and magical writings by examining their shared philosophical foundations rather than treating memory and magic as separate projects. Argues Bruno regarded mnemonic technique as capable of protecting cognition from demonic corruption, and connects memory to deification and the 'heroic spirit.'

Memory

Memory technique regulates and protects the spirits/forces involved in cognition; not separable from magical theory.

Images

The simulacrum is an instrument for regulating cognitive/spiritual forces, richer than a simple 'occult symbol.'

Magic

Magic concerns how images regulate the spirits and forces involved in cognition — protecting the mind, not (only) attracting planetary power.

Seals / Simulacra

Reframes the simulacrum as spirit-regulating rather than merely symbolic.
