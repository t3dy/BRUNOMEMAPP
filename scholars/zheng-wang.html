Zheng Wang

MEDIUM

Interpretation Summary Argues Bruno's image-making belongs directly to his theory of knowledge (gnoseology). Phantasia is not a defective intermediary between sensation and intellect; Bruno's 'shadows' become iconic forms and mental schemas through mnemonic practice, functioning as vehicles for ascent toward divine knowledge. Stresses that Bruno never gives a fully transparent theoretical account of how occult images mediate between worlds — the images must be interpreted as practical realizations of a larger speculative system whose 'code' has not been definitively cracked.

Imagination

Phantasia is a positive cognitive faculty producing iconic forms, not a defective intermediary.

Images

Images are instruments of knowing (iconic practice) — vehicles for ascent from sensation toward divine knowledge, not passive storage devices.
