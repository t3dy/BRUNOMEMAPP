Ioan P. Couliano

1950–1991HIGH

Interpretation Summary Changes what 'magical efficacy' means for Bruno: rather than asking whether the magician literally manipulates supernatural forces, emphasizes the manipulation of phantasia, desire, attention, and the human spirit. Bruno's magical images become a technology of psychological binding (vinculum). Because psyche, imagination, spirit, and the natural world are continuous in Bruno's own framework, manipulating one can affect the others without requiring a choice between 'mere psychology' and 'real magic.'

Imagination

Phantasia is the medium through which psychological/magical binding occurs; central operative faculty.

Images

Images establish vincula (bonds) between magician, imagination, desire, target, and action — a technology of attraction and influence.

Magic

Magical efficacy = psychological binding via phantasia, desire, and attention, intelligible without deciding whether supernatural forces are literally involved.
