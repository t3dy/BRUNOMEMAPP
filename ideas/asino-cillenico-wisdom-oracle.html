The Cyllenic Ass Oracle

COMEDIC_GAME COMEDIC IDEA LOW

A mock-oracular advice tool voiced as the Ass from the Cabala -- dispensing deliberately paradoxical 'asinine wisdom' (profound-sounding nonsense that mimics real oracular/Kabbalistic register) in answer to user questions, with a toggle to reveal the 'serious' Brunian doctrine each joke is parodying.

Why this source supports it: The Ass is Bruno's own chosen vehicle for holding 'divine wisdom' and 'foolishness' together without resolving which is which -- exactly the productive ambiguity a comic oracle needs, and it's already in the source rather than imported.
