Same Bruno, Different Mood

COMPARISON_TOOL PLAYFUL IDEA MEDIUM

A comparison tool that takes a single Brunian concept (e.g. transformation-through-contemplation, or combinatorial symbol-inflation) and shows the SAME underlying move as it appears once played straight (Eroici furori's Actaeon) and once played for laughs (Cabala's Ass) -- making Bruno's range of register itself the object of study.

Why this source supports it: Both works were written within about a year of each other, dedicated to overlapping patrons, using the same combinatorial-symbolic toolkit. The tonal range is Bruno's own, not an app design choice imposed on him.
