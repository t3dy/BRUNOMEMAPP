Plotinus and Bruno: Memory as an Active Power of Soul

How Clucas's Neoplatonic reading reframes the mnemonic wheel as an instrument of ascent

Plotinus rejects the wax-tablet model of memory (perception as passive impression); memory is instead an active power of soul, strengthened by attention and exercise. Clucas argues this is the deep foundation of Bruno's claim that the 'higher art' orders all the operations of the soul, including appetite as well as intellect. The essay traces how Bruno's 'shadows' (umbrae) work as a two-directional structure -- descending from unity into multiplicity in the cosmos, and ascending from images back toward unity in the trained practitioner -- and argues the mnemonic wheel is best understood not as a database or a magical computer but as a model of the soul's movement through a structured universe.
